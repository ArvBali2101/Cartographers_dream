# Submission: Cartographer's Dream

Active project: `merged_game/project.godot` (Godot 4.7, Compatibility renderer).
Local play: `RUN_MAP.bat`. Source checkout: import that project in Godot and press F5.

Four connected playable chapters move from nautical and jungle cartography into a first-person cave and horror labyrinth. Mapping, shifting terrain, keys and pursuit drive the game. Animated narrative scenes and two outcome stories connect the experience. Optional speedrun timing adds replayability.

Latest usability fix: papers have an explicit Finish reading button, keyboard dismissal, paused gameplay and navigable longer notes.

The original merged game's gameplay, art and soundtrack remain the foundation. Researched lore, ancient stone textures, warning/ward detours and a fatal forbidden gate extend it. Asset credits remain in the project. The god reveal is an in-engine stone-idol render, not a literal photoreal organic deity.

This repository is a source submission, not an exported installer. Original video/trailer deliverables are preserved in the sibling archive described in `CLEANUP.md`, not included as oversized source files. Full human balance and cross-device QA remain advisable.

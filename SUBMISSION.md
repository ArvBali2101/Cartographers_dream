# MAP / Cartographer's Dream — submission handoff

## Run

Double-click RUN_MAP.bat from this folder on Windows. Keep the bundled Godot directory beside it. If Windows blocks an unfamiliar downloaded executable, verify its origin before allowing it; do not disable security broadly. The editor launcher is separate from the player launcher.

## What to demonstrate

1. Begin a fresh expedition: the timer is hidden. Move across the ink chart, show a bridge/forest and examine a surviving expedition record. Hold Tab to reveal the route objectives; movement freezes.
2. Explain the central rule: drawing a hidden place creates a connection, and something is learning the route back. F3 summarizes evidence; F1 retains discovered records.
3. Show the nautical chart's soundings, pursuit and a flare holding back tentacles. The three bearings can be visited in any order; the wharf is the exit, not a decorative image.
4. Show a city interior and its impossible knowledge, then the cave's physical sealed entrance and distinct ochre warnings.
5. Show first-person stone corridors, a wooden door physically swinging, the key and red seals. Demonstrate the hunt surge or capture ending without claiming a scripted showcase proves fresh-player balance.

For a short presentation, use a recording from an actual successful playthrough. Do not disguise screenshots as gameplay or promise an unverified completion time. The full game is longer than a two-minute judging demo.

## Concise pitch

“A cartographer falls asleep over his unfinished map. Each dream takes him deeper into the places he thought he was merely recording. But maps work both ways: the things he discovers start finding their way back to him. MAP combines explored ink charts, resource-driven pursuit and a physical first-person maze. Its hidden-by-default timer and stable geography let an atmospheric first playthrough become a route-optimization challenge.”

## Before uploading

- Get the actual competition rules: engine builds, AI assistance disclosure, assets, licensing, team eligibility, file size and judging time. These have not been provided.
- Test on a second Windows machine, with audio and mouse capture. Run through both endings, not only chapter entrances.
- Have a fresh player attempt the whole game. Note unclear objectives, deaths, completion time and difficulty spikes. Automated checks do not replace this.
- Keep README, RESEARCH and source files available. Credit external research; active visuals/audio are procedural and synthesized, not recorded actors.
- Include the playable launcher and engine if binary redistribution/size rules allow, or follow the competition's required Godot export format. This folder is not yet a signed standalone installer.
- Avoid including player save data, unrelated old prototypes or QA screenshots in a submission archive unless required.

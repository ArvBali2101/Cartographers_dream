# Horror research and adaptation

This game tells an original story. These sources inform its construction; they are not a claim that all their mythologies share one canon. No new borrowed passages or audiobook recordings are bundled.

- [H. P. Lovecraft, The Call of Cthulhu](https://www.hplovecraft.com/writings/texts/fiction/cc.aspx): separately collected dream evidence converges on something real. Adapted into the sailors' identical stair drawings and the drowned chart. Cthulhu remains an implication below the water, not an exposition character.
- [H. P. Lovecraft, The Colour Out of Space](https://www.hplovecraft.com/writings/texts/fiction/cs.aspx): environmental contamination makes familiar nature wrong. Adapted into leaves with an unmixable colour and the cave-bound landscape. The prose is a witness's observation, not a diagnosis or scientific assertion.
- [Algernon Blackwood, The Willows](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/11438/11438-h/11438-h.htm): landscape as a thin boundary to beings unconcerned with humanity. Adapted into the unfinished circle, the breathing forest and geography as a two-way crossing.
- [Robert W. Chambers, The King in Yellow](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/8492/pg8492-images.html): a dangerous cultural object carries dread beyond the encounter. Adapted into the childhood atlas, the recurring figure and the warning not to publish the map. We do not assert that escape fixes the connection.

The player's practical goals remain explicit: three sea beacons and docking; the ordered jungle survey and return; traverse the sealed cave; find the labyrinth keys and exit. Optional evidence deepens the mystery without replacing instructions. Cutscene additions can be skipped using the friend's existing controls.

## Named gods update

The story now explicitly names Nyarlathotep, Yog-Sothoth, Azathoth and Shub-Niggurath. Cthulhu is distinguished as a Great Old One rather than labelled an Outer God. These terms are useful game vocabulary, not a claim that Lovecraft wrote a single rigid taxonomy.

- [The Dream-Quest of Unknown Kadath](https://www.hplovecraft.com/writings/texts/fiction/dq.aspx): Nyarlathotep as messenger of the Other Gods and deceptive guide; Azathoth at the centre of chaos. The game's messenger/route connection is an original adaptation.
- [The Dunwich Horror](https://www.hplovecraft.com/writings/texts/fiction/dh.aspx): Yog-Sothoth as a threshold/key presence with time and space connected; Shub-Niggurath is named. The cartographic ritual and leaf evidence are invented for this game.
- [The Haunter of the Dark](https://www.hplovecraft.com/writings/texts/fiction/hd.aspx): Azathoth's blindness and Nyarlathotep's avatar imagery. The game does not assert the common fan claim that reality necessarily ends when Azathoth wakes.

Vane is an unreliable investigator. His identification of a presence is evidence within the story, not an objective lore encyclopaedia. The pursuing beast is not a fully manifested Outer God. The small figure remains unidentified.

## HD assets (continued)

The cutscene figure additionally uses [Gothic Statue by Benny Weimer / Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/gothic_statue), CC0. Build-time retrieval downloads the glTF, geometry buffer and three 2K PBR texture maps and verifies all five MD5 values. It is an ornate stone figure, not a claim of a photorealistic rigged human. Its approach is a camera move with subtle breathing-scale and yaw motion; the unrigged downloaded statue does not have skeletal walking animation. It is an unidentified visual motif, not asserted to be Azathoth's full form.

New outer_gods_drone.wav and outer_gods_pulse.wav are original procedural stereo compositions generated by tools/gen_outer_music.py using the teammate's synthesis helpers. Both are 24 seconds at 32 kHz, 16-bit stereo with -15 dBFS peak normalization. No copyrighted commercial recording was downloaded. Existing soundtrack files remain unchanged; Music adds these layers with the existing music-volume setting. Listening and tuning on real speakers/headphones remains a human QA task.

Downloaded from Poly Haven as 2048-pixel JPEG maps, verified against API MD5 metadata by tools/download_hd.ps1. These are actual surface textures, not website preview renders or AI-generated scenery.

- [Castle Brick 07](https://polyhaven.com/a/castle_brick_07): labyrinth walls; diffuse, OpenGL normal, roughness.
- [Cobblestone Floor 08](https://polyhaven.com/a/cobblestone_floor_08): labyrinth floors; diffuse, OpenGL normal, roughness.
- [Rock Wall 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_wall_02): cave walls; diffuse and OpenGL normal. Existing earth floor and ochre paintings retained.

All these assets use [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). Credit: Poly Haven and its asset contributors. Powered by Poly Haven for build-time asset retrieval. No network requests are made while playing. Original teammate art, music, fonts, level meshes and game systems are retained; existing font license files remain bundled.

Rendering uses Godot Compatibility, not unsupported volumetric effects. Settings offer Performance (no MSAA), HD (2x MSAA) and High (4x MSAA). Window default is 1600x900 with resizable native rendering; fullscreen uses the display resolution. HD does not imply a measured frame-rate guarantee or photorealistic replacement of the illustrated maps.
